# Black & White Theme - Complete ✅

## Color Scheme

### Light Mode
- **Background**: #f5f5f5 (light gray)
- **Cards/Panels**: #fff (white)
- **Text**: #000 (black)
- **Secondary Text**: #666, #999 (grays)
- **Borders**: #e0e0e0 (light gray)
- **Hover States**: #f0f0f0 (lighter gray)

### Dark Mode
- **Background**: #0c0c0c (near black)
- **Cards/Panels**: #1a1a1a (dark gray)
- **Text**: #fff (white)
- **Secondary Text**: #999, #666 (grays)
- **Borders**: #333, #444 (dark grays)
- **Hover States**: #222, #2a2a2a (darker grays)

## Design Principles

- **Minimalist**: Only black, white, and shades of gray
- **High Contrast**: Clear distinction between elements
- **Consistent**: Same color logic across all components
- **Accessible**: Maintains readability in both modes
- **Professional**: Clean, modern aesthetic

## No Colors Used
- ❌ No purple
- ❌ No blue
- ❌ No green
- ❌ No red (except error messages)
- ✅ Only black, white, and grays

## Theme Toggle
Both login and dashboard have theme toggles (☀️/🌙) that switch between light and dark modes while maintaining the black and white aesthetic.
